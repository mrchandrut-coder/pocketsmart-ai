# Team Responsibilities

Assign names before project delivery; individual assignments are not encoded in this repository.

| Role | Responsibilities |
|---|---|
| Project coordinator | Track scope, timeline, risks, and delivery status. |
| Requirements and documentation owner | Maintain requirements, guides, and report consistency. |
| Backend developer | Maintain FastAPI routes, models, configuration, and AI integration. |
| Frontend developer | Maintain templates, browser interactions, and responsive styling. |
| QA and demonstration owner | Prepare test cases, record outcomes, capture evidence, and assemble the demo. |

One person may hold multiple roles for a small team. Confirm names and responsibilities with the project team before submission.
