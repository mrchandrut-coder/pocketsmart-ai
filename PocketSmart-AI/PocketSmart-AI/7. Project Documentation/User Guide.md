# User Guide

1. Start the application and open its local or deployed URL.
2. Choose a planner from the home page or dashboard.
3. Enter the requested budget and category-specific details.
4. Submit the form and review the generated plan, including categories, prices, and tips returned by the AI.
5. Open History to review recent plans while they remain in the current server process.
6. Use the vendor/platform information to independently verify current prices, product details, and availability.

## Planner Inputs

- **Home:** budget, room details, style, shopping platforms, and preferences.
- **Party:** budget, event type, guest count, location, food preference, included services, and notes.
- **Jewelry:** budget, occasion, jewelry types, metal and style preferences, platforms, and optional outfit image.

Recommendations are AI-generated estimates, not quotes or purchase guarantees. Avoid uploading sensitive images or entering private information.
