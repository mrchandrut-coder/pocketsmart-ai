# Problem Statement

People planning home updates, social events, or jewelry purchases often need to compare many options while keeping spending within a fixed budget. Information is spread across vendors and marketplaces, making it difficult to decide how much to allocate to each need.

PocketSmart AI addresses this by collecting a user's budget and preferences and generating an organized, category-specific starting plan. Recommendations are advisory and should be verified against current seller prices and availability.

## Target Users

- Individuals and households planning purchases with a defined budget.
- People organizing events who need a first-pass cost breakdown.
- Shoppers who want style-aware jewelry suggestions, including optional outfit matching.

## Boundaries

The application generates suggestions; it does not purchase products, guarantee prices, reserve venues, or replace professional financial advice.
