# Idea Selection

The selected scope combines three budgeting workflows: home interiors, party planning, and jewelry shopping. Each accepts a budget and preferences, making them a coherent demonstration of AI-assisted personalized planning.

| Idea | Decision | Reason |
|---|---|---|
| Home interior planning | Selected | Produces practical room and product categories from budget and style inputs. |
| Party planning | Selected | Supports event-specific needs and guest-count considerations. |
| Jewelry planning | Selected | Adds occasion and style matching, with optional image input. |
| Direct purchasing and booking | Deferred | Requires commerce, payment, or vendor integrations beyond recommendation generation. |
| Persistent accounts and saved plans | Future work | Current implementation uses in-memory state. |
