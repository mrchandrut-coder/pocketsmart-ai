# Brainstorming Ideas

## Candidate Concepts

- Home furnishing planner that allocates a total budget across rooms and item categories.
- Event planner that estimates categories such as food, venue, and decoration.
- Jewelry recommender based on occasion, metal, and style preferences.
- Optional outfit-image analysis to inform jewelry styling suggestions.
- Recommendation history for reviewing plans during the current server session.
- Platform guidance to help users continue product research.

## Design Principles

- Ask only for inputs relevant to the selected planning task.
- Show the budget and major allocations clearly.
- Treat generated prices as estimates, not live quotes.
- Make external AI and image-processing dependencies explicit.
